## Hong Kong Lands Department map service

The official Lands Department logo is included as browser/vendor/landsd_logo.svg, obtained from https://www.landsd.gov.hk/images/landsd_logo.svg, solely for the required map-source acknowledgement. Road tiles and English labels are fetched on demand from the official map API and are not included in the repository. The map displays the logo and “Map from Lands Department” notice on its face. API documentation: https://tools.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/apidoc/TopographicMapAPI and https://tools.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/apidoc/MapLabelAPI . Service terms: https://tools.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/doc/TNC .
